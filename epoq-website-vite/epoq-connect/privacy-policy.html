Section 01

Overview & Corporate Identity

Welcome to Epoq Connect, a customizable CRM and lead management software platform developed, hosted, and operated by Epoq Zero LLP ("Epoq Zero", "we", "us", or "our"), a limited liability partnership registered in Kerala, India (LLPIN / Registration details maintained under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, India).

Epoq Connect empowers modern businesses, sales teams, educational institutions, healthcare networks, and customer support organizations to organize inquiries, manage customer relationships, and coordinate team workflows within a structured workspace.

We hold customer privacy, confidentiality, and institutional data sovereignty as fundamental pillars of our platform. This Privacy Policy details the exact nature of data handled when you visit our web properties, register for an Epoq Connect tenant, or interact with our software interfaces.

🔒 Our Core Privacy Pledge

Epoq Zero LLP does NOT sell, rent, broker, or monetize your contact lists, CRM records, or inquiry information to third-party advertising networks or data aggregators. Your data remains exclusively yours.

Section 02

Data Controller vs. Data Processor Roles

Under global data protection standards (including the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 [DPDP], and the EU General Data Protection Regulation [GDPR]), rights and obligations depend on whether an entity is a Data Controller (or Data Fiduciary) or a Data Processor:

Context Role of Epoq Zero Role of Client Organization (You) Description of Responsibility
Account Registration & Inquiries Data Controller / Fiduciary Data Principal / Contact Person We determine the means of processing your full name, organization name, work email address, and phone number to provision access and answer inquiries.
CRM Workspace & Lead Records Data Processor Data Controller / Fiduciary Your organization decides which prospective leads, contacts, and notes to enter. We process this data strictly on your documented instructions.

Section 05

Data Isolation & Technical Security Safeguards

We enforce rigorous, multi-layered security protocols to safeguard all tenant information from accidental loss, unauthorized interception, or destructive cyber attacks.

1. Tenant Data Isolation

Epoq Connect enforces strict logical tenant segregation across all database schemas. Every query executed is scoped by unique tenant identification keys, preventing cross-organization data leakage.

2. Cryptographic Controls

  • In-Transit Encryption: All HTTP traffic to Epoq Connect endpoints is forced over modern TLS 1.3 with strict HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS).
  • At-Rest Encryption: CRM databases and backup archives are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Credential Protection: User passwords are encrypted using salted cryptographic hashing algorithms (such as Argon2id or bcrypt).

3. Access Management

CRM accounts support role-based permissions (RBAC) ensuring only authorized members within your organization can access, view, or manage your organization's records.

Section 06

Data Retention & Deletion Schedules

We preserve data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy:

  • Active Account Period: Your organization name, user names, email addresses, and phone numbers remain retained while your organization actively maintains an account with Epoq Connect.
  • Account Inactivity / Termination: Following account closure or formal cancellation, your account data enters a dormant grace period of 30 calendar days, during which your team may request a final export.
  • Permanent Deletion: After 30 days, your account records and tenant data are permanently deleted from live production databases. Backup archives are overwritten on a rolling cycle within a maximum of 90 days.
Section 07

Customer & User Rights (DPDP & GDPR Aligned)

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) and global data protection standards, you hold clear rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You can request a summary of the personal data (name, organization name, email, phone number) associated with your account.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update or correct any inaccuracies in your contact details by contacting us at info@epoqzero.com.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request full deletion of your contact records and tenant workspace.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to register concerns or complaints directly with our designated Grievance Officer.
Section 08

Cookies & Session Audits

Epoq Connect utilizes functional session cookies strictly necessary for application operation:

  • Authentication & Session Cookies: Encrypted, HTTP-only session tokens used to maintain your logged-in state across browser sessions securely.
  • Security Tokens: Unique cryptographic tokens that prevent Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF).
  • Preferences Cookies: Retaining basic user interface preferences, such as theme settings.

We do not install cross-site advertising tracking cookies or ad retargeting pixels within logged-in CRM agent interfaces.
